Cloud migrations succeed when the cutover is boring.

The exciting work happens earlier: inventorying services, containerizing workloads, validating autoscaling, and rehearsing rollback until it feels routine.

## Inventory before ambition

List every dependency that can surprise you: scheduled jobs, object storage paths, secrets, DNS TTLs, and monitoring gaps. Migrations fail on the forgotten glue.

## Optimize after stability

Cost savings from Docker and autoscaling are real, but chasing them mid-cutover is how you invent outages. Stabilize first, then turn the knobs.

## Treat CI/CD as the migration vehicle

If deploys are manual, the migration will be manual forever. Automate the path you will walk during cutover, then walk it repeatedly in staging.
